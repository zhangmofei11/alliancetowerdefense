Tips & Tricks

Essential advice for beginners and advanced techniques for seasoned players.

🌟 Beginner Tips

Getting Started

  • 1.
    Focus on one hero first

    Don't spread resources thin. Level up your best starter hero to max before investing in others.

  • 2.
    Complete daily missions

    Daily missions provide steady coin income and gem bonuses. Always complete all 3 daily missions.

  • 3.
    Play Normal difficulty to grind

    Normal mode offers the best coin-per-minute ratio for progression. Save Hard/Nightmare for when you're stronger.

Unit Deployment

  • 1.
    Watch monster paths

    Before placing units, observe where monsters walk. Place units where they'll get the most hits.

  • 2.
    Upgrade before wave ends

    Use the time between waves to upgrade key units. Plan your upgrades so you're ready before the next wave.

  • 3.
    Don't sell early units

    Early units may seem weak but they build the foundation. You can always add more units later.

🚀 Advanced Tips

Optimization Techniques

  • ★
    Perfect unit spacing

    Place units just outside each other's range to avoid overlap and maximize total coverage area.

  • ★
    Wave skipping

    In late game, use gems to skip easy waves and save time. Each skip costs 10 gems but saves ~2 minutes.

  • ★
    Hero ability timing

    Save hero ultimates for when multiple dangerous enemies are clustered or during boss waves.

Resource Maxing

  • ★
    Gem spending priority

    Gems → Guaranteed S-tier pull (1200) > Unit slots > Energy refresh > Skips

  • ★
    Star upgrade farming

    Run Easy mode repeatedly to farm duplicates. Duplicates are crucial for star upgrades.

  • ★
    Leaderboard grinding

    Play during off-peak hours for easier top 100 placement. Weekend mornings are best.

💎 Pro Tips

🎯

Precise Timing

Activate hero abilities 3 seconds before monsters enter unit range for maximum uptime.

🔄

Rotation Strategy

Keep 2 hero teams rotating. When one uses ultimate, switch to the other for continuous pressure.

🛡️

Shield Management

Arcane Guardian shield lasts 15 seconds. Re-apply 2 seconds before expiry for seamless protection.

⚡

Speed Boost Stacking

Use two Frost Wardens to stack 60% slow effect. Monsters barely move through your kill zone.

💰

Coin Efficiency

A single max-level AoE unit beats three low-level units. Always max primary damage dealers first.

👹

Titan Priority

Titans deal 50x normal damage. If you can't kill one in 10 seconds, you're not ready for Nightmare.
